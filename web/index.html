What the numbers mean

Current bikes and docks come from the operator's public station feed. Experimental risk means the chance of zero bikes or zero docks about 30 minutes later, estimated from 2022 Evanston station snapshots. It has not yet passed a 2026 prospective test. We show no estimate for newer or stale stations.
